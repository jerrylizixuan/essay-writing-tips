General Essay Writing tips

When writing an essay there are a few things to keep in mind that will help make your essay stronger. To start, storm your topic beforehand and make an outline to help structure your ideas. As part of your structure you can create a thesis before you start writing. Having a strong thesis will improve your essay structure and clarity.

All essays follow a similar formate:

  • Introduction
  • Introduction sentences or hook
  • Background information
  • Thesis statement
  • Body Paragraphs
  • Topic sentences
  • Point
  • Example
  • Evidence
  • Repeat point, example, evidence for how many points you have
  • Conclusion sentence
  • Conclusion
  • Restate the thesis
  • Summarize the main points
  • Concluding sentence

Descriptive Essay ▼

Descriptive Essay

Descriptive essays are used to describe objects, the traits and characteristics of people, events, and feelings. The goal is to help the reader picture exactly what is being described. Descriptive writing comes from the author's experiences and perspectives, and should transport the reader to what the author is experiencing. For example, if you are describing roses, you would not only talk about what they look like, but also noticeable details such as sounds, colours, and even the mood or sensation of being around the roses.

Tips for a descriptive essay:

  • Use the five senses
  • Be specific
  • Use figurative language carefully
  • Talk about the origin or if it is an event what led up to it
  • Connect to the readers emotions

Narrative Essay ▼

Narrative Essay

Narrative or personal writing is when the author tells a story from their point of view. There is usually a reason or message behind the story. Narrative essays include characters, a setting, a main event or climax, and most importantly, a clear storyline. They are often written in first or third person.

Tips for a narrative essay:

  • Focus on developing characters
  • Write in third person or first person
  • Try an make use of descriptive writing by using the five senses, connect with emotions, using dialogue strategically, and investigating thoughts
  • A clear topic or point for the essay

Argumentative Essay ▼

Argumentative Essay

Argumentative essay is when you try to convince the readers to believe or support your opinion and topic. You are convincing the readers of a particular argument, not just your opinions. This is done by using evidence which can be, facts, statistics and examples to name a few.

Argumentative Essay Writing Tips

  • Make sure to have a clear Thesis
  • Use descriptive writing and persuasive writing techniques help make your argument:
  • Ethos- convince with celebrities or trust
  • Logos- appeals to logic and facts
  • Pathos- appeals to emotion
  • Have a strong conclusion
  • Make sure to research well

Expository Essay ▼

Expository Essay

Expository essay that explains a topic in a clear and logical way. The purpose is to inform or explain something with facts rather than give one's opinions. Expository writing information, describes, explains, and defines. Thus, the compare and contrast essays, new paper articles, and instruction manuals fall under the expository essay umbrella.

  • Use facts, examples, and explain evidences
  • Do not use descriptive or figurative language
  • Use concise and clear language
  • Support ideas with facts and examples
  • Organize ideas logically
