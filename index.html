Overview

All Essays: Standard Format

  • Introduction: Hook → Background information → Thesis statement
  • Body paragraphs: Topic sentence → Point → Example → Evidence → Explain → Repeat for each point
  • Conclusion: Restate thesis → Summarize main points → Concluding sentence

General Essay Writing Tips

  • Brainstorm your topic beforehand.
  • Make an outline to structure your ideas.
  • Write a strong thesis to improve clarity.
  • Keep each paragraph focused on one main idea.

Essay Types (Fold / Unfold)
Descriptive Essay ▼

What it is

Descriptive essays describe objects, people, events, and feelings. The goal is to help the reader picture exactly what is being described.

Tips

  • Use the five senses.
  • Be specific.
  • Use figurative language carefully.
  • Include origin/background (what led up to it, if it’s an event).
  • Connect to the reader’s emotions.

Narrative Essay ▼

What it is

Narrative writing tells a story from the author’s point of view, usually with a message behind it.

Key features

  • Characters
  • Setting
  • Main event / climax
  • Clear storyline
  • Written in first person or third person

Tips

  • Develop characters.
  • Use senses, emotions, thoughts, and dialogue strategically.
  • Make sure the story has a clear point.

Argumentative Essay ▼

What it is

Argumentative writing convinces readers to support a claim using evidence (not just opinions).

Tips

  • Have a clear thesis.
  • Use evidence: facts, statistics, examples.
  • Use persuasion:
    • Ethos: credibility / trust
    • Logos: logic / facts
    • Pathos: emotion
  • Have a strong conclusion and research well.

Expository Essay ▼

What it is

Expository writing explains a topic clearly and logically. It informs using facts rather than personal opinions.

Tips

  • Use facts, examples, and explain evidence.
  • Use concise and clear language.
  • Organize ideas logically.
  • Avoid descriptive/figurative language (keep it objective).
  • Compare-and-contrast essays often fall under expository writing.
