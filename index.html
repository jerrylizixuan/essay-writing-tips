Essay Writing Tips

Narrative · Descriptive · Expository · Argumentative
Quick Checklist

This site gives clear, school-friendly guidance for four common essay types. Each section includes purpose, structure, actionable tips, and common mistakes.

Narrative Essay

Purpose: Tell a meaningful story that reveals a theme.
  • Structure: Hook → Context → Rising action → Climax → Resolution → Reflection
  • Tips: Focus on conflict and turning points; use concrete actions and sensory details.
  • Common mistakes: A timeline with no tension; details that don’t support the theme.

Descriptive Essay

Purpose: Create a vivid picture of a person/place/object/experience.
  • Structure: Overall impression → Organized details (spatial order) → Closing image
  • Tips: Use specific nouns + strong verbs; choose 3–5 signature details.
  • Common mistakes: Too many adjectives; details with no clear organization.

Expository Essay

Purpose: Explain a topic clearly using facts and examples.
  • Structure: Thesis → Definitions/Background → Explanation/Process → Examples/Impact
  • Tips: Make the logic visible with clear transitions; keep scope controlled.
  • Common mistakes: Dumping information without organization; unclear thesis.

Argumentative Essay

Purpose: Defend a debatable claim with evidence and reasoning.
  • Structure: Claim → Reasons → Evidence → Counterargument → Rebuttal → Conclusion
  • Tips: Use CER: Claim–Evidence–Reasoning; steelman the counterargument.
  • Common mistakes: Opinions with no evidence; weak or unfair counterargument.

60-Second Submission Checklist

  • My thesis is clear and matches the essay type.
  • Each paragraph has one job (one main point).
  • Details/evidence are relevant, and I explain why they support my point.
  • I avoid repetition in the conclusion and end with significance or implications.
  • I checked spelling, punctuation, and sentence clarity.
