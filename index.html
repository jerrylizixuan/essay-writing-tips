Essay Writing Guide

General Essay Writing Tips

When writing an essay there are a few things to keep in mind that will help make your essay stronger. To start, storm your topic beforehand and make an outline to help structure your ideas. As part of your structure you can create a thesis before you start writing. Having a strong thesis will improve your essay structure and clarity.

Essay Structure

  • Introduction
  • Introduction sentences or hook
  • Background information
  • Thesis statement
  • Body Paragraphs
  • Topic sentences
  • Point
  • Example
  • Evidence
  • Repeat point, example, evidence for how many points you have
  • Conclusion sentence
  • Conclusion
  • Restate the thesis
  • Summarize the main points
  • Concluding sentence

60-Second Submission Checklist

  • Make sure your thesis is clear and appropriate.
  • Each paragraph focuses on one clear main idea.
  • Evidence clearly supports the argument.
  • The conclusion avoids repetition and ends with meaningful insight.
  • Proofread for spelling and punctuation.

Narrative Essay

Narrative writing tells a story from the author’s point of view. Narrative essays include characters, setting, and a clear storyline.

Worksheet

Narrative Writing Worksheet

Story Structure

Narrative essays follow a storyline with a beginning, middle, and end. The story introduces characters, setting, and time. A conflict develops and builds through rising action until the climax, followed by falling action and resolution.

Key Takeaways

  • Develop characters
  • Write in first or third person
  • Use sensory description
  • Have a clear story point
